Where do I find the MAC address?
Samsung TV — Settings > General > Network > Network Status > IP Settings
LG TV (webOS) — Settings > All Settings > General > About This TV
Sony / Android TV — Settings > Device Preferences > About > Status
Roku TV / Stick — Settings > Network > About
Fire TV / Stick — Settings > My Fire TV > About > Network
Apple TV — Settings > General > About — "Wi-Fi Address"
Xbox — Settings > General > Network Settings > Advanced
PlayStation — Settings > Network > View Connection Status
Nintendo Switch — System Settings > Internet
Other — Check WiFi / Network settings for "MAC Address" or "Hardware Address"
